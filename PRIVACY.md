# Privacy policy: Setup Planner for Mazévo

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

Setup Planner for Mazévo ("the extension") is an unofficial browser extension that helps event operations staff plan room setups from their own Mazévo Manage Operations page. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Mazévo / Gomazevo.

## What the extension reads

When you use it on your Mazévo site (`*.mymazevo.com`), the extension reads the booking information that your signed-in Mazévo page has already loaded: event names and numbers, buildings and rooms, times, booking status, room setup layouts, ordered services, and the Ready flag. It reads this only to build the setup report you open.

## What it does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any other server. There is no server.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party code.
- It does not read your Mazévo password or sign-in token, and it never asks you to log in to anything.
- It does not change anything in Mazévo. It only opens pages and moves Mazévo's day view forward and back, as you would by hand.
- It does not run on any website other than `*.mymazevo.com`.

## What is stored, and where

These are kept only in your own browser, in the extension's local storage:

- your settings (furniture rules, view, and whether the hourly refresh is on)
- rooms you tick as done
- a copy of the last bookings seen for each day, used to show what changed since you last checked
- the address of your Mazévo site

Ticks and copies older than 21 days are deleted automatically. Removing the extension deletes all of it. Nothing is synced or shared. Copy, CSV and Print only happen when you click them, and the result stays on your computer.

## Permissions

- **Access to `*.mymazevo.com`:** needed to read the bookings shown on your Mazévo Manage Operations page and to add the "Plan setups" button there.
